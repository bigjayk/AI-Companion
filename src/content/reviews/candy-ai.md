Candy AI has built its reputation around image and short-video generation rather than pure conversation depth. Its standout feature, Live Action, produces short animated clips of your chosen companion — something few competitors offer at this price point. The platform runs as a browser-based app rather than a native mobile download.

Text chat is unlimited on any paid tier, but images, video, and voice run on a separate token system — worth knowing up front, since light users can stay near the base price while heavy media users often land closer to $25–$50/month.
